Welcome to Goa

Goa is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India. It is famous for its beaches, beautiful scenery and relaxing atmosphere.

About Goa

Goa is located on the western coast of India. It is known for its beaches, Portuguese architecture, churches and vibrant culture.

Popular Places

Baga Beach

Baga Beach is famous for water sports, restaurants and nightlife.

Fort Aguada

Fort Aguada is a historic fort with beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

Dudhsagar Falls

Dudhsagar is a beautiful waterfall surrounded by greenery.

Famous Food

Goa is also famous for delicious seafood, fish curry, rice and traditional local dishes.
